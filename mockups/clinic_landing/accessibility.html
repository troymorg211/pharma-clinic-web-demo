Accessibility

Care that works for everyone.

We want everyone to be able to use this website and visit our studios comfortably. Here is what we have built in, and how to tell us if something gets in your way.

On this website

  • Works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator and a “skip to content” link.
  • Semantic headings, landmarks and labelled form fields for screen readers.
  • The booking dialog keeps focus inside while open, closes with Escape and returns focus to where you were.
  • Text and interface colours chosen to meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
  • Respects your device’s “reduce motion” setting.
  • Layouts that reflow down to 320px wide and support 200% zoom.

At our studios

  • Step-free access and accessible toilets at both studios.
  • Hearing loop at Harbour Studio reception.
  • Free interpreters, including British Sign Language, on request.
  • Quiet rooms if you find waiting areas difficult — just ask.

Tell us what would help

If anything on this site or in our studios is hard to use, call 020 7946 0123 or email hello@astercare.example. We can also provide information in large print, easy read or other formats.

This is a concept site; this statement describes its design intent, not the result of a formal audit.
