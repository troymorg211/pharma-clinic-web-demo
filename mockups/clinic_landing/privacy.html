Privacy · demo

A transparent prototype, with no real data handling.

This page explains the limits of the Aster Care demonstration. It is not a production privacy notice.

What happens here

Interactions stay in your browser.

The contact, newsletter and booking forms are static interface demonstrations. They validate entries locally to show an interaction state, but do not submit, transmit, retain or share information.

No cookies, analytics services, trackers, appointments, patient records or medical advice services are implemented in this demo.

A production website needs a practice-specific privacy notice, a documented data flow, secure hosting and an appropriate legal and accessibility review before it collects personal information.

For a live practice

Replace this page before collecting any information.

Use approved practice contact details, data-retention information, third-party processor disclosures and the rights that apply in the practice’s location.
