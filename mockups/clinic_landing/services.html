Care services

A patient-friendly route through the services a practice offers.

These sample pathways are designed to be adapted and clinically reviewed. They do not provide medical advice or replace an assessment by a healthcare professional.

General careDental carePharmacyWomen’s healthDiagnostics

Urgent or emergency concern? This demo cannot assess needs. Use your local emergency services or an appropriate emergency care provider.

01 / General care

Begin with a question.

Example entry point for routine care, a new non-emergency concern or planning a follow-up conversation.

People may start here for

  • Routine planning
  • A new question to discuss
  • Follow-up coordination

What to expect

A real practice can explain visit length, preparation, fees and next steps in its own approved terms.

02 / Dental care

Plan your dental visit.

Use this route to make dental service options and visit preparation easy to understand.

People may start here for

  • Routine dental care
  • A concern to discuss
  • Planning a return visit

What to expect

Publish only practice-approved information about how dental visits are arranged and what patients should bring.

03 / Pharmacy & wellness

Practical support, clearly explained.

A pharmacy can use this path to introduce approved services and provide a direct way to ask about practical next steps.

People may start here for

  • Service information
  • Wellness conversation
  • Collection planning

What to expect

Individual medicine questions need a pharmacist or other suitable healthcare professional.

04 / Women’s health

Care with context.

An adaptable route for practices that offer women’s health appointments and ongoing conversations.

People may start here for

  • Preventive-care discussion
  • Appointment planning
  • Follow-up questions

What to expect

A practice should set out its own services, availability and appropriate preparation guidance.

05 / Diagnostics

Understand the next step.

A structured place to explain diagnostic coordination, preparation and result discussions in the terms a practice approves.

People may start here for

  • Test coordination
  • Preparation questions
  • Results discussion planning

What to expect

Requirements vary. Talk with your healthcare professional about your individual situation.
