Services & prices

Everything you need, clearly priced.

Book directly with the right clinician. Every fee is shown before you confirm, and there are no membership charges.

01

GP appointments

Appointment£79
Length20 minutes
WithDr Amara Okafor, Dr Sofia Marin, Dr Tom Ellery

Same-week appointments for new symptoms, ongoing conditions, fit notes and referrals — in person or by video.

Common reasons to book

  • New or changing symptoms
  • Long-term conditions such as asthma or blood pressure
  • Fit notes and referral letters
  • Repeat prescriptions and reviews

What to expect

A 20-minute appointment with a GP, in person or by video. You will receive a written summary afterwards, and any tests or referrals are arranged before you leave.

02

Dental care

Appointment£95
Length45 minutes
WithDr Lewis Hart, Maya Costa

Check-ups, hygiene visits and treatment planning with a calm, unhurried team who explain every step.

Common reasons to book

  • Routine check-ups and X-rays
  • Hygiene and gum health appointments
  • Toothache or a broken filling
  • Second opinions and treatment plans

What to expect

A 45-minute visit that includes an examination and a clean. Any treatment is discussed and priced in writing before it starts.

03

Pharmacy & prescribing

Appointment£25
Length15 minutes
WithPriya Nair

Speak to a prescribing pharmacist about everyday conditions, repeat medicines and medication reviews.

Common reasons to book

  • Minor illnesses such as sore throats and skin rashes
  • Medication reviews
  • Questions about side effects
  • Help managing several medicines

What to expect

A 15-minute consultation with a prescribing pharmacist. Where appropriate, they can issue a prescription the same day.

04

Women’s health

Appointment£110
Length30 minutes
WithDr Amara Okafor, Dr Sofia Marin

Contraception, menopause, period and fertility conversations with clinicians who give you time.

Common reasons to book

  • Contraception advice and reviews
  • Menopause and perimenopause support
  • Period problems
  • Pre-conception conversations

What to expect

A 30-minute appointment with a GP with a special interest in women’s health. You are welcome to bring someone with you, or to request a chaperone.

05

Blood tests & health checks

Appointmentfrom £45
Length15 minutes
WithDr Amara Okafor, Dr Tom Ellery, Jonah Reyes

Quick, gentle blood tests and health checks, with results explained by a clinician — not just emailed.

Available tests

  • General health and wellbeing panels
  • Tests requested by your GP or specialist
  • Cholesterol, thyroid and diabetes checks
  • Follow-up and monitoring bloods

What to expect

A quick appointment with our practice nurse. Most results are back within 48 hours and a clinician will explain them to you.

06

Travel health

Appointment£35
Length20 minutes
WithPriya Nair, Dr Tom Ellery, Jonah Reyes

Personal travel risk assessments, vaccines and antimalarial advice, planned around your itinerary.

What is covered

  • A personal travel risk assessment
  • Vaccines, priced individually
  • Antimalarial advice and prescriptions
  • Advice on food, water, insects and sun

What to expect

A 20-minute consultation, ideally six to eight weeks before you travel. Bring your itinerary and any previous vaccine records.
