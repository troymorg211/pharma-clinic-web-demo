Visiting us

Practical details.

How do I change or cancel an appointment?

Use the link in your confirmation email, or call us. Changes are free up to 24 hours before your appointment.

What should I bring?

Photo ID if you are new to us, a list of any medicines you take, and your insurance details if you are claiming. For travel health, bring your itinerary.

Can I bring someone with me?

Of course. You can also request a chaperone for any examination — just ask when you book or when you arrive.

Is there parking?

Garden Rooms has six free spaces, including one accessible bay. Harbour Studio is a four-minute walk from the station, with paid parking nearby.

Do you offer interpreters?

Yes. Let us know the language you need when you book and we will arrange a telephone or in-person interpreter at no cost.
