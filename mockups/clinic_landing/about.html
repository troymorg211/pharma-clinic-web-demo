About Aster Care

A practice built around time to talk.

We started Aster Care because we wanted to practise medicine the way we would want it for our own families: unhurried, joined-up and honest about what things cost.

Harbour Studio, opened 2019

Our story

Small enough to know you. Big enough to help.

Aster Care opened its first studio on Quay Street in 2019 with two GPs and a pharmacist. Today we are a team of seventeen across two sites, offering GP, dental, pharmacy and nursing care under one roof.

We have kept the things that mattered from day one: appointments long enough to talk properly, clinicians who follow up on their own results, and prices published openly.

We also work closely with local NHS services, so if you need care we do not provide, we will help you get to the right place.

What we believe

Four promises we keep.

Time is part of the treatment

Rushed appointments lead to missed details. Our standard GP appointment is twenty minutes, and you can always book longer.

Plain language, always

We explain what we are thinking, what the options are and why — then write it down so you can read it again at home.

One team, joined up

Your GP, pharmacist and dentist can talk to each other. Fewer repeated stories, fewer things falling through the gaps.

Honest about cost

Every price is online. If something will cost extra, we tell you before we do it — never after.
